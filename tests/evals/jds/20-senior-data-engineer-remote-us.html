Senior Data Engineer

Remote - United States · Full-time · Engineering

About Tidewater Data

Tidewater Data helps shipping companies predict port congestion. We are 90 people, fully remote, and profitable.

What you will do

  • Design and run streaming and batch pipelines on Kafka, Spark and Snowflake
  • Improve data quality checks and lineage across the platform
  • Mentor two mid-level engineers and review designs
  • Partner with data scientists to productionise forecasting models

What you bring

  • 6+ years of experience in data engineering
  • Expert SQL and Python
  • Hands-on experience with Spark and a cloud data warehouse
  • Experience with Airflow or Dagster
  • Nice to have: Terraform, experience with geospatial data

Compensation and benefits

The base salary range for this role is $140,000 - $165,000 USD per year. We also offer equity, a home-office stipend, 401(k) match and unlimited vacation.

Apply before January 31, 2027.

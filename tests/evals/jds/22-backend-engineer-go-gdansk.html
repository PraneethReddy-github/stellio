Backend Engineer (Go)

Gdańsk, Poland - hybrid (2 days on site) - full-time

Kestrel Robotics builds warehouse robots. The fleet-management backend coordinates thousands of robots in real time and we need another engineer to grow it.

Responsibilities

  • Build and operate Go services that schedule robot tasks
  • Design APIs (gRPC and REST) for the warehouse operators' UI
  • Improve observability and performance of the message pipeline (NATS)

Requirements

  • 3+ years of backend development, ideally in Go
  • Strong knowledge of PostgreSQL and distributed systems basics
  • Good written and spoken English

Nice to have

  • Experience with robotics or logistics software
  • Kubernetes

Salary: 18,000 - 26,000 PLN gross per month (B2B or employment contract).

# Cover letter rules

These rules are given to the AI on every cover letter generation. Truth rules still apply: every claim comes from the profile.

## Layout (done by code and the template)

Your name, postal address (Profile → Address; else your location), telephone and e-mail at the top; the company and its location; "Place, DD.MM.YYYY" and "Application for the position of <role>" in bold; then the salutation, the body, the closing, a signature line and your name.

## Structure

The body answers four questions in a cohesive text, connected to your previous work: how you found the company, why you want to work for them, why they should choose you, and what you want to learn from them.

1. **How and why (one short paragraph):** where you found the posting (only from <job_source>; say nothing about it when it is not recorded) and one specific reason for this company and role, taken from the JD or your notes. Start with substance, not with "I am writing to…".
2. **Why you (1–2 paragraphs):** two or three concrete achievements from the profile that map directly to the JD's top requirements. Name the requirement implicitly through the achievement; don't list requirements back.
3. **What you want to learn, and close (one short paragraph):** what you want to learn there, grounded in what the JD says the team does; logistics (location, work authorization, start date) only when relevant; a plain request for a conversation. No begging, no superlatives.
4. Keep the letter to one page.

## Style

- Plain, direct, confident. Short sentences. Active voice.
- Sounds like a capable person wrote it in 20 minutes, not like a template.
- Use the hiring manager's name if the JD gives it; else the fallback salutation.
- Mention the company by name at most 3 times.
- No repeating the resume line by line; add context the resume can't (why, how, what you learned).
- English by default; match the JD language only if the rules say so.

## Never

- Never invent facts, motivation stories, or company knowledge not in the JD or your notes.
- Never use the banned phrases.
- Never exceed one page.
